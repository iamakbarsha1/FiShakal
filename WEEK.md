# FiShakal — Weekly Menu & Prep

Same daily skeleton every day (see `PLAN.md` §3). Only the protein rotates. Target: ~1,800 kcal, ~160 g protein.

---

Protein lands between 150–170 g every day. Calories land between 1,700–1,900 except Friday (~2,100) and Sunday (~2,200) — the weekly average still works out to roughly 1,850.

**If you fast Mon/Thu:** those two days come in around 1,400–1,500 kcal naturally, which offsets Friday and Sunday completely. Suhoor = 3 eggs + curd + 2 chapati. Iftar = dates + water, then the normal dinner with protein doubled.

## Friday, specifically

Biryani day in most Tamil Muslim households, and fighting it is a losing battle. Plan it instead:

- **1.5 cups of biryani rice**, served onto your plate once. Not a refill, not "a little more".
- **Double the chicken.** Ask for an extra piece. Protein is what makes it a meal and not a carb dump.
- **Raita + raw onion + lemon** on the side. Volume and fibre, near-zero calories.
- **Skip the evening snack.**
- **Light dinner** — curd rice with an egg, or just soup and salad.
- Walk 40 minutes that evening.

Done this way, Friday lands at ~2,100 kcal. That's a 250 kcal overshoot, which the week absorbs without noticing.

## Sunday batch prep — 90 minutes

Do this and the weekday plan runs itself. This is the single habit that decides whether the plan survives past week three.

1. **Boil 12 eggs.** Refrigerate. Breakfast and snacks are now solved for 4 days.
2. **Set curd** from 1 L milk. Cheaper than buying and gives you ~5 servings.
3. **Boil and squeeze 300 g soya chunks**, store in the fridge in an airtight box. Keeps 4 days. Now a soya sundal is a 5-minute job, not a 30-minute one.
4. **Soak kondakadalai** overnight Sunday → pressure cook Monday morning → sundal for 3 evenings.
5. **Chop vegetables** for 3 days of poriyal. Store in boxes.
6. **Clean and portion the week's chicken/fish** into 150 g bags, freeze. Thaw one the night before.
7. **Roast and grind 250 g flaxseed**, store in a jar. 1 tsp daily into curd or kanji.

## Quick recipes you'll actually repeat

**Soya sundal (5 min, ~175 kcal, 26 g protein)**
Pre-boiled squeezed soya 50 g dry weight → temper mustard, curry leaves, green chilli, a little grated coconut → toss → lemon and salt. Keeps in the fridge.

**Egg + keerai poriyal (10 min, ~250 kcal, 20 g protein)**
Sauté onion and garlic in ½ tsp oil → add chopped keerai → wilt → scramble in 3 eggs → salt and pepper. One pan, cheap, high volume.

**Sardine curry (20 min, ~220 kcal per portion, 30 g protein)**
Standard meen kuzhambu, half the usual oil, tamarind, no coconut paste. Sardine is oily enough on its own. Tastes better the next day.

**Ragi kanji (5 min, ~200 kcal)**
2 tbsp ragi flour whisked into water, boil 3 minutes, salt or buttermilk. Breakfast when you're running late. Mix in 1 tsp flaxseed.

**Chicken + soya curry extender**
Cook your normal chicken curry with 100 g chicken and 50 g dry soya instead of 200 g chicken. Same volume on the plate, more protein, ₹25 cheaper per portion, and the soya soaks up the masala so it tastes like more chicken, not less.
