# FiShakal — Fat Loss Plan

**Profile:** Male, 25, Chennai, 171 cm, 86 kg, desk job (software).
**Started:** 6 Oct 2026
**Constraints:** Halal. Home-cooked Tamil Muslim food (family cooks, you cook sometimes). Budget ₹4,000–6,000/month. No known medical conditions.

---

## 1. Where you are

| Metric                            | Now                              | Target      |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Weight                            | 86 kg                            | 70–72 kg    |
| BMI                               | 29.4 (overweight, edge of obese) | 24          |
| Waist (measure at navel, morning) | measure it today                 | under 90 cm |

Waist matters more than the scale number. For South Asian men, above 90 cm at the navel is the line where visceral fat starts causing problems, and it drops before the scale does. Measure it on day one or you lose your only good progress signal.

## 2. The numbers

- **BMR** (Mifflin-St Jeor): 1,810 kcal — what you burn lying in bed all day.
- **TDEE**: ~2,350 kcal — desk job, light walking.
- **Daily target: 1,800 kcal.**
- Deficit: ~550 kcal/day → **0.5–0.6 kg/week**.

### Macros

|         | Grams     | kcal | Why                                                               |
| ------- | --------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Protein | **160 g** | 640  | Keeps muscle while losing fat. Keeps you full. Non-negotiable.    |
| Fat     | **50 g**  | 450  | Floor for hormones. Mostly from eggs, fish, coconut, cooking oil. |
| Carbs   | **180 g** | 720  | Rice, idli, chapati, dal. Enough to train and function.           |

Protein is the one number to hit. If you hit 160 g protein and roughly 1,800 kcal, the rest sorts itself out.

**Reference points:** 1 egg = 6 g protein. 100 g cooked chicken = 27 g. 100 g dry soya chunks = 52 g. 1 cup curd = 7 g. 1 cup cooked rice = 200 kcal, 45 g carbs, 4 g protein.

## 3. Daily structure

Same skeleton every day. Only the protein source rotates (see `WEEK.md`).

| Time  | Meal                                                                                          | kcal      | Protein   |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- | --------- |
| 7:00  | Black coffee or tea, no sugar                                                                 | 5         | 0         |
| 8:00  | **Breakfast** — 2 idli + sambar/chutney + 3 eggs (boiled, or omelette in ½ tsp oil)           | 450       | 28 g      |
| 11:00 | **Snack** — 200 ml curd (unsweetened) + 15 g peanuts                                          | 200       | 12 g      |
| 13:30 | **Lunch** — 1 cup rice + 150 g chicken/fish/soya curry + big bowl poriyal or keerai + rasam   | 530       | 49 g      |
| 17:30 | **Evening** — soya or kondakadalai sundal + black tea no sugar                                | 200       | 20 g      |
| 20:30 | **Dinner** — 2 chapati (wheat or ragi) + 150 g fish/egg/dal curry + cabbage or cucumber salad | 450       | 40 g      |
|       | **Total**                                                                                     | **1,835** | **149 g** |

Add one more egg or an extra 150 ml curd to reach 160 g protein.

### Swaps inside the skeleton

- **Breakfast:** 2 idli ↔ 1 dosa ↔ 1 cup ragi kanji ↔ 1 cup upma (small) ↔ 40 g oats in water + cinnamon.
- **Lunch rice:** 1 cup white rice ↔ 1 cup kambu/samai millet ↔ 2 chapati. Millet is not magic; it just has more fibre and keeps you full longer.
- **Dinner chapati:** 2 chapati ↔ 2 ragi dosa ↔ ¾ cup rice.
- **Protein:** see the rotation in `WEEK.md` and the per-rupee table in `FOOD.md`.

## 4. The family-food problem (your actual hard part)

Your food gets cooked for everyone, not for your plan. You can't rewrite the menu. You can control three things:

1. **Proportion on your plate.** Half the plate vegetables, a quarter protein (meat/fish/dal/soya), a quarter rice. Serve yourself — don't let the plate get served to you.
2. **Rice portion.** One cup, served first and no refill. This single change is worth about 300 kcal/day. Serve rice onto your plate _before_ the curry so you can see the amount.
3. **Two small asks at home.** Ask for (a) less oil in your portion — ladle from the top of the curry, not the oil layer at the bottom; (b) one extra egg or an extra piece of fish/chicken set aside for you. Both are easy asks. Don't ask the household to go low-carb or cook separately — it won't hold.

Everything else you add yourself: eggs, curd, soya sundal, salad. These are cheap, need no cooking skill, and need no one's permission.

## 5. Rules

**Daily**

- 160 g protein. Count it loosely for the first two weeks until you know what it looks like.
- Zero sugar in tea and coffee. This alone is 100–200 kcal/day for most people.
- 3 litres water. Chennai heat makes thirst feel like hunger.
- 8,000–10,000 steps. Walk after dinner.
- 7+ hours sleep. Short sleep raises hunger hormones and stalls fat loss more than most diet mistakes.

**Weekly**

- Weigh once a week: same day, morning, after toilet, before eating. Track the number, but judge by the **4-week trend**, not week to week. Water weight swings 1–2 kg for no reason.
- Measure waist same day.
- One planned free meal. Biryani, parotta, dawat, whatever. Planned, not stolen.

**Cut entirely (these are the Chennai-specific calorie bombs)**

- Sugar in drinks, bakery items (puffs, cake, karachi halwa, roast), sugarcane juice, falooda, badam milk, fruit juice, soft drinks.
- Parotta and bun parotta. One parotta is ~250 kcal of refined flour and dalda and you never eat one.
- Evening bajji/bonda/samosa habit.
- Shawarma with mayo (the mayo is most of the calories — ask for no mayo and it becomes a decent meal).

**Keep, with control**

- Biryani: once a week, 1.5 cups, double the chicken, add raita and onion salad, skip the evening snack that day and keep dinner light.
- Mandi/grills: actually fine. Grilled meat is the best thing on an Arabian menu. Eat the meat, go light on the rice, skip the mayo-garlic sauce.
- Dates: 2–3, not a handful. Good around training.

## 6. Nutrients beyond calories

You asked for complete nutrition, not just weight loss. This is how each one gets covered on this plan:

| Nutrient              | Source in this plan                                                     | Watch out                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Protein**           | Eggs, soya, fish, chicken, dal, curd                                    | Covered                                                                                                          |
| **Iron**              | Liver once a week, keerai, dal, dates                                   | Vegetarian iron absorbs poorly — eat it with lemon/tomato (vitamin C)                                            |
| **B12**               | Eggs, fish, meat, milk                                                  | Covered as long as you eat animal food most days                                                                 |
| **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** | Sardine (mathi), mackerel (kavalai), nethili — 3× a week                | These cheap small fish beat expensive fish for omega-3. Flaxseed powder (1 tsp daily) helps but is a weaker form |
| **Calcium**           | Curd, milk, ragi (ragi is 344 mg/100 g — higher than milk), sesame/ellu | Covered if you keep ragi and curd in                                                                             |
| **Vitamin D**         | Sun + eggs + fish                                                       | **Most likely gap.** Indoors all day = deficiency. See below                                                     |
| **Fibre (30 g+)**     | Veg, dal, millets, whole fruit                                          | Ramp up slowly or you'll bloat                                                                                   |
| **Potassium**         | Banana, coconut water, keerai                                           | Covered                                                                                                          |
| **Zinc**              | Eggs, meat, dal                                                         | Covered                                                                                                          |
| **Iodine**            | Iodised salt                                                            | Covered, just use iodised salt                                                                                   |

**Supplements — only these three, and only if needed:**

1. **Vitamin D3** — get a blood test first. If low (most desk workers in Chennai are), the standard correction is 60,000 IU weekly for 8 weeks, then maintenance. Cost: ~₹10/sachet. Ask a doctor, don't self-dose long term.
2. **Whey protein** — optional convenience, not required. ~₹2,000/kg. Only buy it if you're consistently missing 160 g protein from food. Soya chunks give you the same protein at a third the price.
3. **Nothing else.** Fat burners, detox teas, apple cider vinegar, green coffee — all waste.

**Worth doing once, at the start (~₹1,200–2,000 at any Chennai lab):**
CBC, fasting glucose + HbA1c, lipid profile, liver function (LFT), TSH, Vitamin D, B12. At BMI 29 with a desk job, fatty liver and pre-diabetes are common enough that it's worth knowing. Not mandatory to start the plan — start now, test when you can.

## 7. Fasting — use what you already have

Mondays and Thursdays are a natural fit and give you a free deficit without any extra planning. If you fast those two days:

- **Iftar:** dates (2–3) + water first, then your normal dinner with the protein doubled. Don't open the fast on fried snacks — that's where the calories go.
- **Suhoor:** 3 eggs + curd + 2 chapati or oats. Protein and fibre, not just carbs, or you'll be starving by noon.
- A fasted day lands around 1,400–1,500 kcal naturally. Two of those a week speeds things up by roughly 0.1–0.15 kg/week.
- Don't train heavy on a fasting day. Walk instead.

Ramadan 1448 starts around early February 2027. Revisit the plan in January — Ramadan needs its own structure, and the goal there is to _not gain_, not to cut hard.

## 8. Training

You haven't decided yet. Here's the ranking, honestly:

**Non-negotiable, start today:** 8,000–10,000 steps/day. Walk 30 minutes after dinner. Costs nothing. This is most of your extra burn.

**Highest leverage thing you can add:** lifting weights 3× a week. In a calorie deficit without resistance training, roughly a quarter of what you lose is muscle. You'll hit 70 kg and still look soft — "skinny fat". Lifting keeps the muscle so the weight you lose is fat.

| Option                       | Cost                        | Verdict                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Gym, 3×/week                 | ₹600–1,200/month in Chennai | Best. Worth more than any food upgrade you could buy with the same money |
| Adjustable dumbbells at home | ₹1,500–3,000 one-time       | Good. Works if you'll actually use them                                  |
| Resistance band              | ₹300                        | Okay to start. You'll outgrow it in 2 months                             |
| Bodyweight only              | ₹0                          | Works for the first few months, then stalls                              |

**If you start: 3-day full body, 45 minutes each.** Squat (or goblet squat), push-up or bench press, row, overhead press, Romanian deadlift, plank. 3 sets of 8–12 each. Add weight or reps every week. That's the whole program — you don't need more than that for the first six months.

Decide by the end of week 2. The diet works without it, but you'll like the result less.

Roughly 6–7 months to 72 kg. That pace is slow on purpose — faster cutting costs you muscle and almost always rebounds.

**If weight hasn't moved in 2 consecutive weeks** (judging by weekly average, not a single reading): cut 150 kcal (one less cup of rice) _or_ add 2,000 steps. Change one thing, wait two weeks, reassess. Don't slash calories — that's how people end up eating 1,200 kcal and stalling anyway.

**If you're losing more than 1 kg/week:** eat more. You're losing muscle.

## 10. First week — do only this

Don't change everything at once. Week 1:

1. Measure weight and waist. Write them down.
2. Cut sugar from tea/coffee and stop all sweet drinks.
3. Add 3 eggs at breakfast every day.
4. One cup of rice at lunch, no second serving.
5. Walk 30 minutes after dinner.

That's it. Week 2, add the curd snack and the soya sundal. Week 3, run the full day structure. Week 4, decide on training.

Five changes you actually keep beat twenty you abandon by Friday.
